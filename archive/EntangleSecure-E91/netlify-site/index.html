The E91 protocol

From entangled pairs to a shared key

01 · GENERATE

Create a singlet pair

A source prepares two particles in an entangled state and sends one to Alice and one to Bob.

02 · MEASURE

Choose settings at random

Alice and Bob independently choose measurement directions and record their outcomes.

03 · TEST

Check Bell correlations

They disclose a subset of settings and estimate CHSH S. Eavesdropping can weaken the violation.

04 · DISTILL

Keep or abort

If checks pass, compatible rounds can be processed into a key; otherwise they abort.

Scope: This Netlify bundle is a static educational demo. It does not include the Python app’s full density-matrix engine, finite-sample measurement records, Cascade error correction, Toeplitz privacy amplification, PDF/CSV reports, or reproducible run history. The original app remains in the project folder.
